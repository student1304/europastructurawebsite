About Us

The purpose of the Foundation is to conduct independent research and promote knowledge dissemination in the fields of technology and finance, with a particular emphasis on the European Union and its capital markets. The Foundation's research focuses on the impact and potential of emerging technologies—such as artificial intelligence, quantum technologies, and digital infrastructures—on financial systems, regulation, and policy development.

The Foundation seeks to enhance the education and informed decision-making of European policymakers, public institutions, and other stakeholders through its research, publications, and collaborative initiatives.
